  • Requirements, fees, deadlines and procedures in Germany change. We work to keep our guides accurate and current, but you must confirm anything that matters against the official source — the German mission handling your application, uni-assist, the APS office, DAAD, or the relevant university or authority — before you act on it.

4. Paid services booked outside this site

Two services are paid. Both are booked and paid on Razorpay payment pages, which you reach through the link in our YouTube channel bio, not on this website. The terms shown on each booking page apply alongside these terms. Where the two differ, the one more favourable to you applies.
