        Read this before you act on it. This test measured what you recognise on a page. It did not hear you speak, and speaking is where almost everyone sits a step lower. Before booking any Goethe exam, practise producing the language out loud with someone who will correct you: recognising a structure on a page is not the same as producing it under time pressure.         How the result is worked out

        Ten questions per level, climbing from A1

        1. 10 questions each

          A1, A2 and B1, in that order.

        2. 7 of 10 counts as solid

          High enough that guessing between four options doesn't reach it, low enough that one careless slip doesn't drop you a level.

        3. You start where it stops being solid

          The result walks up from A1 and stops at the first level that isn't yet solid.
