Where should you start in German?

Thirty questions, about five minutes. You get one answer per question, then a starting point and what to work on next — with a tip after every question either way.

30 questions A1 · A2 · B1 ~5 minutes

What this is, honestly: a reading and grammar test. It shows what you recognise, which is not the same as what you can say. Your speaking level is usually a step below your reading level, so treat the result as where to start — not as your CEFR level.
