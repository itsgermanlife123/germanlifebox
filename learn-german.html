01 · German learning roadmap

Your path
from A1 to B1.

Each level is a step up. Class hours follow the Goethe-Institut course plan (75-hour intensive courses); add your own practice on top.

03 · Certification, the proof

The Goethe exam,
four modules.

The Goethe-Institut certificate is recognised worldwide by universities, employers and visa offices. Four components: Speaking, Listening, Reading and Writing. Knowing the four components in advance is half the preparation.

Hören · Listening40min

Announcements and conversations: catch the key messages and important details.

Lesen · Reading65min

Different kinds of texts: key information, details, viewpoints and opinions.

Schreiben · Writing60min

Personal and formal e-mails, plus your opinion in a forum post.

Sprechen · Speaking15min

A pair exam: plan something together, give a short talk, answer questions.

Durations are for the Goethe-Zertifikat B1. The modules can be taken on their own or together. Goethe-Institut India ↗

With consistent study (1–2 hours per day), most people reach B1 in 12–18 months. If you study 3+ hours a day, some reach it in 8–10 months. For scale, the Goethe-Institut course plan puts B1 at roughly 375 hours of class, before your own practice. Speaking practice accelerates this significantly, because speaking forces your brain to retain vocabulary differently than reading does.

It depends on the programme. English-taught programmes usually require IELTS / TOEFL instead of German. German-taught programmes usually require at least B2, and many ask for DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 (around C1). A Studienkolleg asks for at least B1, some for B2. Check the language requirement on each university's own programme page.

A1 is foundational: most people find it manageable. A2 is where grammar starts getting complex (cases, word order, modal verbs). B1 is where many learners get stuck: longer sentences, nuanced vocabulary, and speaking fluently under pressure. This is exactly why speaking practice matters at B1 level.

Yes. The four modules of the Goethe-Zertifikat B1 (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking) can be taken individually or in combination, so you can resit only the module you missed. Check with the institution you're applying to whether it accepts module certificates from different dates.

No. For an Ausbildung visa, recognised B1 certificates such as Goethe, telc or ÖSD are common.
