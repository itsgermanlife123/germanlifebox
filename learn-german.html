Everyday German

Past tense, modal verbs, shopping, travel, health, and daily life topics. You can hold short conversations and understand simple texts. A2 is where German starts to feel real.

Independent Speaker — University Ready

Complex topics, opinions, travel, work, and study contexts. You can understand the main points of clear texts and hold conversations on familiar topics. B1 is the minimum most German universities require — and what most visa applications need.

Don't Make This Mistake While Learning German!
How long does it take to reach B1 from zero?+
With consistent study (1–2 hours per day), most people reach B1 in 12–18 months. If you study 3+ hours a day, some reach it in 8–10 months. Speaking practice accelerates this significantly because speaking forces your brain to retain vocabulary differently than reading does.
Do I need German to apply to German universities?+
It depends on the program. English-taught Master's programs usually require IELTS/TOEFL instead of German. German-taught programs require B1 or B2. For most Bachelor's programs, B2 or C1 is needed. Check the language requirement on each university's own programme page.
What's the difference between A1, A2, B1 in terms of difficulty?+
A1 is foundational — most people find it manageable. A2 is where grammar starts getting complex (cases, word order, modal verbs). B1 is where many learners get stuck — longer sentences, nuanced vocabulary, and speaking fluently under pressure. This is exactly why speaking practice matters at B1 level.
Can I prepare for the Goethe exam without living in Germany?+
Absolutely. The Goethe-Institut has exam centres in India (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Pune, Bengaluru). You take the exam there. The roadmap on this page is designed to prepare you for those centres — same format, same timing, same pressure.
