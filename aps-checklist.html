The basics

What APS is, and why it matters

APS — the Academic Evaluation Centre, run under the German Embassy in New Delhi — verifies that your Indian academic documents are genuine. That's its only job. It doesn't grant admission and it doesn't approve your visa. It just confirms your marksheets and certificates are real.

But it's a required first step for most Indian applicants: most German universities won't process your application without it, and you generally can't complete your student visa without it either. Skip it or delay it, and your whole timeline slips with it.

Check this first

Are you eligible?

For Bachelor's applicants specifically, there's a threshold worth knowing before you go any further:

📌 The 70% rule

From Winter Semester 2026/27 onward, both Bachelor's routes require 70% or higher overall in Class XII (any board). Completing a Bachelor's year in India does not substitute for the 70% — it's an additional requirement on top of it, not an alternative route around it. Confirm on APS India's official update ↗

Your situationWhat this usually means
Class XII 70%+ (any board)Eligible for the Studienkolleg pathway — subject-restricted admission via a foundation year. Proceed
Class XII 70%+ and 1 completed Bachelor's year in IndiaEligible for the direct pathway — subject-restricted admission to the same or a closely related field. Not automatic: the university still makes the final call. Confirm
Class XII below 70%Neither Bachelor's pathway is currently open under the new criteria — a completed Bachelor's year doesn't change this. Confirm
Completed Bachelor's degree (Master's applicants)Standard graduate APS procedure — the 70% Class XII rule doesn't apply here. Proceed

Special cases — distance/online degrees in restricted fields, private university programs without full accreditation, or alternate entrance qualifications — are genuinely assessed individually. I'd rather tell you to confirm directly than guess and get it wrong for your specific case.

Get ready

Before you start

A few things to sort before you register:

  • Your passport is in hand and valid
  • Your academic documents (X, XII, Bachelor's, and Master's if applicable) are complete
  • Your Aadhaar is linked to your current mobile number, if your procedure requires it
  • You know which APS procedure applies to you — Bachelor's, Master's, or Undergraduate — since the checklist varies slightly by procedure
The full list

Document checklist

Ten documents make up a complete APS application. APS only starts processing once your application is complete — a single missing document is enough to delay everything, so go through this slowly.

01APS Application Form
Printed, personally signed, with a passport-size photo attached. Your signature must match your passport exactly — this trips people up more than you'd expect.
02Passport Copy
First and last pages, clear and fully readable. Check your passport is still valid before you start — an expired one stops everything.
03APS Fee Payment Receipt
Proof of payment. The fee is non-refundable once paid (details in the next section), so don't submit until the rest of your documents are genuinely ready.
04Aadhaar Card If applicable
A clear copy, used for identity verification under the current procedure.
05Class X Documents
Marksheet and passing certificate.
06Class XII Documents
Marksheet, passing certificate, and admit card if it's available for your procedure.
07Bachelor's Documents Mandatory
All semester marksheets, plus your Degree Certificate — or a Provisional Degree Certificate if that's what you have, as long as it's still within the validity APS specifies. This is the document set most graduate applicants get tripped up on, mainly because they're missing one semester's marksheet.
08Master's Documents If applicable
Degree certificate and transcripts, only if you've already completed a Master's.
09Language Certificate
Copies of whichever apply to you — IELTS, TOEFL, Goethe, TestDaF, or TELC. APS asks for German and/or English certificates where relevant to your application. Note: this is separate from dMAT (see below) — don't confuse the two.
10Student Authorization Letter Mandatory
Download the current version directly from the official APS India Checklists page ↗, fill it in, sign it, and include it. This is the single most-forgotten document on this whole list — it's easy to miss because it's not academic paperwork like everything else.
Cost & time

Fee & processing time

APS Fee
₹18,000
Non-refundable once paid. Pay by card, UPI, or netbanking via CCAvenue — it's faster and easier to track than a bank transfer, which relies on manual matching and can add delay. Confirm on aps-india.de ↗
Processing Time
Variable
APS India states it cannot guarantee a fixed processing time for any individual case — it depends on application volume and how quickly your school, board, or university responds to verification requests. Many applicants see results in a few weeks; peak season can take considerably longer. Start as early as you can — it's the one part actually in your control. Confirm on aps-india.de ↗
Step by step

How to actually apply

The document checklist tells you what to gather. This is how it actually gets submitted:

1
Confirm your procedure type
Bachelor's, Master's, or Undergraduate — the checklist and portal registration differ slightly by procedure.
2
Register on the APS India portal
Create your account and start your application online before anything gets couriered.
3
Pay the ₹18,000 fee
CCAvenue is faster to track. Keep the receipt — it's one of your 10 required documents.
4
Complete the Application Form & Authorization Letter
Two separate documents. Sign both exactly as your passport signature.
5
Register for dMAT separately, if it applies to you
dMAT is run by a different organisation (g.a.s.t., not APS India) — see the section below for who this actually affects.
6
Scan and organise everything
Clear A4 colour scans of every document, kept as one combined backup PDF (see Bibin's folder method below).
7
Courier your complete physical application
APS India, Gate No. 3, DLTA Complex, R.K. Khanna Stadium, 1 Africa Avenue, New Delhi 110029. Verify on aps-india.de ↗
8
Track your application online
Use the portal to check status rather than guessing — it's updated as your file moves through verification.
9
Receive your certificate
Delivered by post once verification is complete. Keep both the physical copy and a scan safe — you'll need it again at multiple points later.
New for 2026

The dMAT update — what actually applies to you

APS introduced the Digital Master Test (dMAT) in June 2026 — a new academic aptitude test that sits alongside your APS documents, but only for specific applicants. It's run by g.a.s.t., a separate organisation from APS India — a genuinely common mix-up, since people assume it's the same office.

⚠️ Who this affects

It applies to you only if you're applying for a Master's in Engineering, Business/Management, Commerce, Accounting, Finance, or Economics, for Summer Semester 2027 or later. Full eligibility list on aps-india.de ↗

You do NOT need it if:

  • You're applying for Winter Semester 2026/27 — the first dMAT results aren't out until October 2026, after most Winter deadlines pass
  • You registered for APS online, or shipped your complete documents, before 29 June 2026
  • You already hold an APS certificate
  • You're applying for a Bachelor's or PhD, or your field isn't in the list above

If it does apply: it's computer-based, around 3.5 hours, in two parts — a core academic module and a subject-specific module — taken in person at a licensed test centre. The fee is €150, separate from your APS fee.

Worth knowing: right now, only RWTH Aachen and Göttingen actually factor dMAT scores into admission decisions, for specific programs. If it applies to your field, you'll still need to take it — but for most universities, it won't be the deciding factor in whether you get in. Check your specific target university's current requirements directly, since this is a fast-moving area. See the official dMAT site ↗

One more distinction worth making: dMAT is not TestAS. TestAS is a separate, older aptitude test some universities use independently — the two aren't related, and taking one doesn't cover the other.

Avoid these

Common mistakes

  • ✕Sending incomplete documents. APS only processes complete applications — anything missing often leads to delays or requests for additional information.
  • ✕A signature that doesn't match your passport. Use the exact same signature everywhere.
  • ✕Blurry photocopies. APS wants clear, legible A4 colour copies — not phone photos.
  • ✕Forgetting the Authorization Letter. The most commonly missed document on the whole list.
  • ✕Applying too late. Processing times stretch during peak season — start as early as you can.
  • ✕Sending originals. Only send what the checklist actually asks for — usually copies, unless APS specifically requests an original.

Still wondering

Frequently asked questions

Does APS interview applicants?+
Rarely, and only for specific cases — for example, if you're applying while still completing your Bachelor's. When it happens, it's brief (around 20 minutes), focused on your academic history, and you're notified in advance. Most applicants never get one.
How long is my APS certificate valid?+
Generally treated as indefinitely valid for most purposes. If it's been several years since yours was issued, it's worth confirming directly with APS before relying on it for a fresh application.
Do I need IELTS or TOEFL for APS itself?+
No — APS verifies your academic documents, not your language ability. Universities separately require language certificates as part of admission, which is a different process.
What if my degree was via distance education or a private university?+
These are assessed individually by APS, especially for restricted fields or programs without full accreditation. Don't assume either way — confirm your specific program's status directly.
Can I fix a mistake after I've submitted?+
Generally no — once your documents are couriered, correcting an error usually means starting the process again. This is exactly why the review step before shipping matters as much as it does.
